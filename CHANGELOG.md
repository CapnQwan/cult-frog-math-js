## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Vector types `Vec2`, `Vec3` and `Vec4`, with `ReadonlyVec2`, `ReadonlyVec3` and `ReadonlyVec4`, and the `Vec2`, `Vec3` and `Vec4` namespaces with `create`, `clone`, `set`, `copy`, `isEqual` and `isEqualEpsilon`, plus `isEqualXY`, `isEqualXYZ` and `isEqualXYZW` respectively to compare against loose components.
- Vector arithmetic on `Vec2`, `Vec3` and `Vec4`: `add`, `sub`, `mul`, `div` and `scale`. `mul` and `div` work per component, and `div` follows normal float rules, so a zero divisor gives `Infinity` or `NaN` rather than a guarded value.
- Vector measurement on `Vec2`, `Vec3` and `Vec4`: `magnitudeSq`, `magnitude`, `distanceSq`, `distance` and `normalize`. `normalize` turns a zero vector into a zero vector rather than `NaN`.
- Vector products: `dot` on `Vec2`, `Vec3` and `Vec4`, and `cross` on `Vec3`. `cross` follows the right-hand rule.
- Vector helpers on `Vec2`, `Vec3` and `Vec4`: `scaleAndAdd`, `negate`, `lerp`, `reflect` and `angle`. `scaleAndAdd` writes `a + b * scalar`, `lerp` is unclamped, like the scalar `lerp`, `reflect` expects a normal with a length of `1`, and `angle` returns radians and gives `0` for a zero vector.
- Per-component vector helpers on `Vec2`, `Vec3` and `Vec4`: `abs`, `min`, `max`, `floor`, `ceil` and `round`. `min` and `max` compare two vectors and can mix components from both, `floor` rounds toward negative infinity, and `round` rounds halves toward positive infinity, like the scalar `round`, but takes no `decimals`.
- Packed reads on `Vec2`, `Vec3` and `Vec4`: `fromPacked` copies one row of a tightly packed array into `out` without making a view. The index is a row, not an element offset, and bounds aren't checked, so a component read from outside the array is written as `NaN`. Each namespace also gets a `SIZE` constant.
- Vector constants: `ZERO` and `ONE` on `Vec2`, `Vec3` and `Vec4`, `UP`, `DOWN`, `LEFT` and `RIGHT` on `Vec2` and `Vec3`, plus `FORWARD` and `BACK` on `Vec3`. The 3D axes are y-up and right-handed, so `FORWARD` is negative z and `BACK` is positive z.
- Matrix types `Mat2`, `Mat3` and `Mat4`, with their `Readonly*` variants, and the `Mat2`, `Mat3` and `Mat4` namespaces with `create`, `clone`, `set`, `copy`, `identity`, `isEqual` and `isEqualEpsilon`, plus `IDENTITY` and `SIZE` constants. Matrices are stored column major; `create` and `set` take their arguments in row-major order.
- Quaternion type `Quat` and `ReadonlyQuat`, and the `Quat` namespace with `create`, `clone`, `set`, `copy`, `identity`, `isEqual`, `isEqualEpsilon`, `isEqualXYZW`, `multiply`, `magnitudeSq`, `magnitude`, `normalize`, `conjugate` and `fromAxisAngle`, plus `IDENTITY` and `SIZE` constants. Quaternions are stored as `[x, y, z, w]`, and `create()` gives the identity rotation, since `w` defaults to `1`. `multiply(out, a, b)` composes rotations, applying `b` first and then `a`, like column-vector matrices, and does not renormalise. `normalize` turns the zero quaternion into the identity, unlike the vector `normalize`, which keeps a zero vector at zero. `conjugate` is the inverse only for unit quaternions. `fromAxisAngle` follows the right-hand rule and expects a unit axis, which it does not normalise. Equality compares components, so `q` and `-q` are not equal even though they represent the same rotation.
- Linear colour type `Color` and `ReadonlyColor`, and the `Color` namespace with `create`, `clone`, `set`, `copy`, `isEqual`, `isEqualEpsilon`, `isEqualRGB`, `isEqualRGBA` and `lerp`, plus `SIZE` and the constants `WHITE`, `BLACK`, `RED`, `GREEN`, `BLUE`, `YELLOW`, `CYAN`, `MAGENTA` and `TRANSPARENT`. Colours are stored as `[r, g, b, a]` with linear channels from `0` to `1`. Nothing clamps, so values above `1` are valid HDR channels, and colours are not premultiplied. `create` defaults alpha to `1`, while `set` requires it. `isEqualRGB` ignores alpha, and `lerp` is unclamped.
- Packed colour type `Color32` and the `Color32` namespace with `create`, `red`, `green`, `blue`, `alpha` and `lerp`, plus the same named constants as `Color`. A `Color32` is an sRGB-encoded 8-bit RGBA colour packed into one unsigned number as `0xRRGGBBAA`, so it compares directly against a CSS-style hex literal. `create` rounds each channel to the nearest integer and then wraps it to 8 bits, with no clamping, and defaults alpha to `255`. `lerp` blends the encoded values, then clamps each channel to `0`–`255`.
- Colour conversions: `Color.fromColor32` decodes a `Color32` into `out` with the sRGB transfer function, and `Color32.fromColor` encodes a `Color` back. Alpha is only scaled, never encoded. `fromColor` saturates every channel to `0`–`1` first, so HDR values clip to `255` rather than wrapping and negative channels become `0`. `NaN` channels are packed as `0`. Every `Color32` survives a round trip through `Color`, and the named constants match exactly in both namespaces.
- Scalar helpers: `clamp`, `saturate`, `round`, `wrap`, `snapToZero` and `isEqualEpsilon`.
- Interpolation helpers: `lerp`, `invLerp`, `smoothstep` and `smootherstep`. `lerp` is unclamped, while the others clamp their result to `[0, 1]`.
- The `Easing` namespace, with `easeIn`, `easeOut` and `easeInOut` on `Easing.Quad`, `Easing.Cubic`, `Easing.Quart` and `Easing.Quint`, plus `smoothstep` and `smootherstep` on `Easing.Smooth`. Every curve takes a single `t` and does not clamp it. Available from the `@cult-frog/math/easing` subpath and the package root.
- Constants: `EPSILON`, `EPSILON_SQUARED`, `PI` and `TWO_PI`.
- A subpath export per type (`@cult-frog/math/vec3`, `@cult-frog/math/mat4`, `@cult-frog/math/quat`, `@cult-frog/math/color`, `@cult-frog/math/color32`, ...), each exporting its namespace and types. The package root exports all of them.
- All values except `Color32` are branded `Float32Array`s and can be views over either an `ArrayBuffer` or a `SharedArrayBuffer`. `Color32` is a branded number.
